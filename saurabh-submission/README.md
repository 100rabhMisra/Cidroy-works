# Submission

This folder is where you place your completed written work. Copy the six files from
[`docs/candidate/submission-template/`](../docs/candidate/submission-template/) into
this folder and write them up — the layout inside each is yours.

## What to include

Six written artifacts:

- `01-code-review.md` - your review of the two supplied branches.
- `02-refund-design.md` - your end-to-end refund flow (sequence diagram) and your
  delivery plan.
- `03-architecture-review.md` - your architecture review and target-state extension.
- `04-build-impact-note.md` - the impact note for your Part 3 fix.
- `05-ai-usage.md` - a candid account of how you used AI (framed as transparency;
  it is not held against you).
- `06-questions-and-next-steps.md` - your open questions, assumptions, where you
  stopped, and what you'd do next.

Your Part 3 code and its test stay in the normal module and test directories, not
in this folder. In the root `README.md`, link to the implementation commit and
record the exact test command. Do not add credentials, generated secrets,
database volumes, or any material not supplied to you.

Before submitting, verify from a clean clone that the documented startup and test
commands work.

## The discussion round

A seventy-five to ninety minute discussion follows your submission. We walk
through your review and design, ask you to defend specific decisions, and run a
short live extension of the work. Its purpose is to confirm you understand what
you submitted - however or whatever helped you produce it. Come ready to explain
your reasoning, not to recite your document.
